# DayPay

**DayPay records what I actually earned, so payday is never a guess.**

A day-rate work tracker for people whose pay is a formula, not a salary — built mobile-first as an installable PWA that works offline.

## What it does

- **Log a day in one tap** — weekday (`OK`), weekend (`2×`), overtime (`OT`), holiday (`HOL`, auto-detected for Nigeria) and paid/unpaid leave types, each stamped on the calendar
- **Effective-dated rate history** — a raise only affects days logged from its start date; every stored amount is frozen on the day it was logged, so the past is never rewritten
- **Payday projection** — projected total at payday, days remaining, monthly/yearly goal progress
- **Month & year views** — previous months lock with their final salary preserved
- **Shares & payslips** — WhatsApp text and PDF exports that tell the truth under mixed rates (`4 days = ₦70,000`, not a false uniform rate)
- **Export my data** — one-tap JSON backup (every record and setting) and CSV (one row per worked day)
- **Four looks** — Light, Dark, Glass · Dark, Glass · Light; money stays green in all of them
- **Quiet by design** — plain saves pop their cell; the celebration card is saved for weekends, OT, holidays and milestones

## The money rule

Every amount in DayPay — hero, folds, shares, payslips, exports — is the stored amount for that day, not what today's rate would imply. *Final salary preserved* is real.
